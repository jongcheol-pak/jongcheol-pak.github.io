# Pilot's Manual

## Controls
- **Arrow Keys**: Movement
- **SPACE**: Fire Photon Torpedoes
- **Z**: Barrel Roll

## Enemies
1. **Scout**: Fast, low health.
2. **Cruiser**: Slow, heavy armor.
3. **Mothership**: RUN.

> "Fly safe, commander."
