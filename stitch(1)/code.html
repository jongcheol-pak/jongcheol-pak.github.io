01 Overview

High-performance, real-time visualization tool designed to bridge the gap between abstract mathematical concepts and visual intuition.

Built on a custom-designed rendering engine, it allows researchers and developers to observe the weight adjustments and activation patterns of complex neural architectures as they learn.

Whether you are debugging a vanishing gradient or demonstrating backpropagation to students, Neural Net Sim provides the clarity required for deep technical insights. The system handles million-parameter models with sub-millisecond latency.
